Graceful Degradation

    The crate

    Earlier pressings with copies left. When a record sells out, it isn't pressed again.

    How drops work

    1. 1Sign in before the drop

      Use your email or your Google account. Your cart and orders follow you to any device.

    2. 2Add up to three of each

      Copies aren't held in your cart. Stock goes down only when an order is placed.

    3. 3Check out

      If checkout fails, nothing is charged and you can try again straight away.
